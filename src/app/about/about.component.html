About Tom & Jerry Caraccioli

Tom Caraccioli and Jerry Caraccioli are identical twin brothers and three-time authors: Ice Breakers; BOYCOTT: Stolen Dreams of the 1980 Moscow Olympic Games and regional bestseller STRIKING SILVER: The Untold Story of America's Forgotten Hockey Team. The brothers also served as editors and advisors for the memoir of broadcasting pioneer and Pro Football Hall of Famer Lesley Visser, Sometimes You Have to Cross When it Says Don't Walk.

Along with writing books, the brothers contribute a column for USA HOCKEY Magazine and serve as contributing writers to the Capital Region's 55+life Magazine, Oswego County Business Magazine, CNY 55 Plus Magazine (Central New York) and The B Magazine (Berkshires). They also host a monthly television and multi-platform segment on WSYR-Channel 9 (ABC) in Syracuse, NY – Backroads to Bridge Street with Tom & Jerry.

The brothers grew up in upstate Oswego, New York.

Career Highlights

Jerry Caraccioli

Jerry spent 25 years as a member of the CBS Television Network Sports Division's Communications Department serving as its Executive Director, Communications. He currently works in education as the creator and developer of a Sports Management Program.

Among his career highlights, Jerry worked for CBS Sports on some of the biggest events in sports including the 1998 Olympic Winter Games in Nagano, Japan, eight Super Bowls, 24 American Football Conference Championships, multiple NCAA Division I Men's Basketball Championships and Final Fours, as well as U.S. Open Tennis Championships, the Masters® and PGA Championship. He also has worked in Major League Baseball for the California Angels and Oakland Athletics organizations, as well as Major League Baseball International as the Director of Media Relations for the Australian Baseball League in Sydney, Australia.

Tom Caraccioli

Most recently, Tom served as Vice President of Communications for Kroenke Sports & Entertainment (Outdoor Channel, Sportsman Channel, World Fishing Network). He also was a member of network communications department teams at NBC Sports, USA Network, Turner Sports and Outdoor Sportsman Group Networks.

Among his career highlights, Tom's position at NBC Sports/USA Network involved working some of the most prestigious events in sports television (Olympic Summer Games, Masters®, Ryder Cup, U.S. Open Tennis Championships, French Open Tennis Championships and more). While at Turner Sports, he worked events in partnership with the NBA, MLB, NCAA Final Four, PGA Tour, PGA of America, NASCAR and others. He started his career in Major League Baseball for the Boston Red Sox in various capacities in the organization including work with The Jimmy Fund of Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, was the Director of Sports Information at Merrimack College in Andover, Massachusetts, and a managing editor at Professional Sports Publications in New York City.
